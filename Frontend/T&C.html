Demonstration & Showcase Prototype Disclosure

✦
⚠️ IMPORTANT PROTOTYPE NOTICE
Restaurant Management System is a software demonstration prototype built strictly for technical portfolio showcase, UI/UX evaluation, and proof-of-concept testing. This website is not an active commercial enterprise, operating restaurant, or registered merchant. No real products, services, or food items are offered, fulfilled, or delivered through this platform.
SECTION 01

Prototype & Demonstration Purpose

All components of this web application—including menu listings, table reservation floor plans, payment gateways, and administrative dashboards—serve exclusively as interactive mockups.

  • Designed strictly for demonstration and technical display.
  • No commercial orders or physical reservations are processed.
  • System features may undergo live testing or updates without notice.
SECTION 02

Mock Orders & Services

Items added to the cart, submitted orders, or scheduled reservations do not initiate real-world restaurant operations or food preparation.

  • Prices, dishes, and stock numbers are simulated data.
  • Table seating grids demonstrate interactive UI capabilities only.
  • No staff or kitchen receives actionable fulfillment requests.
SECTION 03

Payment & Financial Disclaimer

Payment flows shown on this platform (including UPI QR code simulations, UTR entry, and sandbox gateway configs) operate strictly in demonstration or test mode.

  • Do NOT enter real sensitive financial data (e.g., bank PINs, CVVs, or live credentials).
  • No real money will be charged or deducted.
  • Simulated transaction approvals generate mock receipts only.
SECTION 04

User Data & Privacy for Testers

Any information entered into test forms (such as names, emails, or sample messages) is stored transiently in local development databases for feature testing.

  • Please use dummy data when interacting with test forms.
  • Data is not harvested, sold, or shared with commercial entities.
  • Sessions and temporary test data may be cleared periodically.
